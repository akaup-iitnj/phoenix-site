About

We run one.

Phoenix Industrial Labs was started in Pleasantville, New Jersey, to give young people a real way into skilled manufacturing work, and to give the programs that train them a facility worth the name. America needs technicians faster than it is producing them, and the surest way we know to make one is a classroom that manufactures: real machines, a real product, and a curriculum that puts students on the floor by their third semester. We build that facility, we run one ourselves, and we deliver it as one package.
